# Forge Research OS

## Objective
Build useful practitioner intelligence that compounds. Forge's advantage is not generic AI output; it is structured context, evidence, comparables, follow-up, and institutional knowledge.

## Research loop
QUESTION → CAPTURE → STRUCTURE → VERIFY → ANALYSE → SYNTHESISE → PUBLISH → FEEDBACK → COMPOUND

## Evidence discipline
Separate facts, participant statements, inference, hypothesis, and recommendation. Preserve provenance. Do not invent missing data. Flag weak evidence rather than filling gaps with confidence.

## Practitioner loop
Practitioner participation → anonymous structured data → useful insight → publish findings → attract further participation → stronger dataset and comparables.

## Anonymity
Default to collecting only information needed for the research purpose. Do not expose identifiable practitioner or school information in public outputs without explicit permission. Aggregate where practical.

## Analysis
Look for patterns, exceptions, trade-offs, context variables, and alternative explanations. Avoid treating correlation as causation. Small samples are directional, not representative benchmarks.

## Publication
A useful Forge insight should answer: What are we seeing? How strong is the evidence? Why might it matter? What should a practitioner consider doing? What do we still not know?

## Capture
Any repeated decision pattern, useful taxonomy, validated question, comparable, or failure mode should be considered for the Forge intelligence schema so the research asset compounds.
